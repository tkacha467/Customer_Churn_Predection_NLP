Behavioral Feature Extraction

Transactional details are synthesized into behavioral features:

  • Recency: Days since the last order.
  • Frequency: Count of unique orders.
  • Monetary: Cumulative amount spent.
  • Operational Features: Delivery delays, payment installments, and average review scores.
Churn Definition

A customer is labeled as **Churned** if they have ordered exactly once and have been inactive for over **180 days**.

Context-Aware Fusion Engine

Robust Context-Aware Analysis

Instead of forcing reviews into binary classes, we implement an advanced pipeline:

  • 3-Class Sentiment Classification Differentiates between POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, and NEUTRAL text dynamically using RoBERTa.
  • Sarcasm Fusion Modifier Detects sarcastic phrasing (e.g. "Five stars for breaking immediately 🥱") and re-weights the scores.
  • Neutral Integrity Rule Neutral reviews (e.g., "Arrived on Monday") are mapped to `No Strong Sentiment` and are never falsely flagged as suspicous.
